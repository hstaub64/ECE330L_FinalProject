# ECE330L_FinalProject

The hardest part of this project was understanding the SysTick handler
and the layered display method. Creating the maps became intuitive after
looking at the drawing on the board, but since we've had little practice 
with the SysTick handler as a whole, it was difficult to understand
where to put these methods, even once we understood the necessity
of it for the layered design method. Plenty of outside resources and
help from classmates was used to understand the steps, and once the
wireframe was added via comments and method stubs, it became easier
to fill them in.
